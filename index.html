Skin tips

Simple habits for healthier skin

Good basics go a long way. For persistent problems, please see a dermatologist.

Do I really need sunscreen every day?

Yes. Daily sunscreen of SPF 30 or higher helps prevent tanning, dark spots and early ageing, even on cloudy days and at higher altitudes where UV is stronger.

How should I wash my face?

Use a gentle cleanser twice a day with lukewarm water. Avoid harsh scrubbing, and follow with a moisturiser suited to your skin type.

Should I squeeze or pick pimples?

No. It can spread infection and leave lasting marks and scars. If acne keeps coming back, it is best to have it treated.

When should I see a dermatologist?

See a doctor if a rash, breakout, patch or hair problem lasts more than a few weeks, keeps returning, spreads, or if a mole changes in size or colour.
